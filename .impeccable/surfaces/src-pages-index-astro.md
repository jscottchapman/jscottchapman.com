## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Vienna Secession showroom lattice. Every skill, case, and note sits in a ruled square cell, and the newest skill gets the biggest cell. It refuses the category default: a cream blog column, a stock hero, a card grid, a newsletter band.

OWN-WORLD: Gallery white #F4F4F1 ground, lacquer black #0F0F0F rules and fills, palladium #8C9096 hairlines, pewter #C4C7CB. Marcellus SC letterspaced capitals for display; Jost in sentence case for body. Square-pierced border strips, corner squares on frames, every control a square cell that fills solid black when active. One slow silver glint along rules is the only motion.

STORY: A reader sees four free skills, tries a real lettered Calculus Tutor step, opens a skill to read, copy, or download its source, and may sign up. A client sees outcome numbers in fixed cells and writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Lattice nav bar on top. Left frame: the name in large capitals, "Free Claude skills, built for my own work", the bio, two square-cell buttons. Right frame, equal height: Calculus Tutor with the example step, answers A to D. The other three skills begin as a row of cells at the fold.

FORM: Wiener Werkstätte gridsmith showroom, lead of the bolder re-roll (round 1), not on my grounded list. Seed key 778aa424.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
